School Portal Website

A dynamic and fully functional school portal designed to streamline student access to academic resources, profiles, and events. The portal integrates Firebase authentication and Firestore database to manage user accounts securely.

Key Features

  • User Profiles: Students can create and update their profiles, including photos, bio, and personal details.
  • Gallery Section: Students can upload photos, videos, or text posts. Admin approval ensures content moderation.
  • Interactive Comments & Likes: Users can comment, reply, and like posts with profile pictures displayed alongside interactions.
  • Event Management: Upcoming school events with countdown timers; expired events are auto-hidden.
  • Role Management: Admin can promote users to moderators and transfer ownership securely.
